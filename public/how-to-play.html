How To Play

What HoopDuels Is

HoopDuels is a live 1v1 NBA teammate-chain game built around players from 2000 to today.

Every duel starts from a random NBA All-Star, and each correct guess moves the chain to a new current player.

How a Turn Works

  1. Name a real NBA teammate of the current player.
  2. If your guess is valid, that player becomes the new current player.
  3. Your opponent then has to continue the chain from there.

Fouls

  • Unknown player
  • Player was not a teammate of the current player
  • Player was already used earlier in the chain

How to Win

  • Your opponent fouls out after 3 fouls
  • Your opponent runs out of their 60-second turn timer

Ranked ELO

Signed-in account vs account games are ranked. Wins move your ELO up, losses move it down, and your profile tracks peak ELO, best win, streak, and progression over time.

Practice Mode

Practice uses the same chain rules, foul rules, and 60-second timer, but it does not affect ranked ELO.

Example Chain

LeBron James → Kyrie Irving → Kevin Durant → James Harden

Each player must have been teammates with the one immediately before them.
