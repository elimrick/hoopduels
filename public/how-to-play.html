How To Play

Background

HoopDuels is a live 1v1 NBA teammate-chain game.

Player pool is 2000 to present, and each game starts from a random All-Star.

Playing HoopDuels

  1. Name a teammate of the current player.
  2. If correct, your guess becomes the new current player.
  3. Turns alternate back and forth.

Fouls

  • Unknown player = foul
  • Not a teammate = foul
  • Repeated player = foul

How to Win

  • Opponent fouls out (3 fouls).
  • Opponent runs out of the 60-second turn timer.
