How to Play

The Basics

Every duel starts with a random NBA All-Star. The player pool is made up of all NBA players from the 2000-01 season to present. Keep the chain moving by naming a teammate of the current player before your time runs out.

How to Win

Each player starts with 90 seconds. You win when your opponent runs out of time or reaches 3 fouls.

What Counts as a Foul

Guessing an incorrect teammate or leaving the game tab during either player’s turn adds one foul to your total.

No Repeat Players

Each player can appear only once in a chain, including the starting player. A repeat guess counts as a foul.

Rating System

Create an account to play ranked duels. Friend games and duels against guests do not affect your rating.
