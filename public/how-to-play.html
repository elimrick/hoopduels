How To Play

The Goal

Keep the teammate chain going longer than your opponent.

On Your Turn

  1. You have 60 seconds.
  2. Name a real NBA teammate of the current player.
  3. If correct, your guess becomes the new current player.

Fouls

  • Unknown player
  • Not a teammate
  • Already used in the chain

How You Win

  • Your opponent gets 3 fouls
  • Your opponent runs out of time

Ranked ELO

Signed-in account vs account games change your ELO. Practice mode does not.

Player Pool

HoopDuels uses NBA players from 2000 to today, and every duel starts from a random All-Star.

Example Chain

LeBron James → Kyrie Irving → Kevin Durant → James Harden

Each name must have been teammates with the player right before it.
