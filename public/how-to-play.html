How To Play

The Basics

Every duel starts with a random NBA All-Star. The player pool is made up of all NBA players from 2000 to present. Keep the chain moving by naming a teammate of the current player before the time runs out. Climb the leaderboard to prove you know ball.

How to Win

Opponent either runs out of time in a turn or reaches 3 fouls.

What Counts as a Foul

Guessing a player who was not teammates with the current player.

Rating System

Create an account to have a rating. Duels against guests have no impact.
