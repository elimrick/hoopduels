Privacy Notice

Accounts and gameplay

HoopDuels is operated by Ewan Limrick. We store your username, a salted password hash, the date you joined, and your game statistics, match history, friendships, and friend requests to run the game and save your progress. Your username, rating, and record can appear on the leaderboard. Opponents can see your username during a duel and in their match history. Friends can see your online status.

Browser storage and signup protection

HoopDuels stores sign-in and game information in your browser. We use IP addresses to limit repeated signup and sign-in attempts. Render hosts HoopDuels and its database. Cloudflare Turnstile processes browser and connection information to verify signup attempts.

Account deletion

You can delete your account from your profile. This removes your profile, friendships, and head-to-head friend records from the active database. Other players’ match histories may still contain your username and past match results.

Hosting retention

Render keeps application logs for 7 days under our current hosting plan. Deleted account data may remain in Render’s database recovery backups for up to 3 days.

Privacy requests

HoopDuels is intended for a general audience. If you have a privacy request or believe a child under 13 provided personal information through the site, email hoopduels@outlook.com.
