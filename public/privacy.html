We may also disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, enforce site rules, or protect the rights, safety, and security of HoopDuels or its users.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, and comply with legal requirements.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Use the site with that understanding.
