Terms of Use

The game

HoopDuels is an independent basketball trivia game. It is unaffiliated with the NBA or its teams.

Fair play

Play your own games. Do not use bots to create accounts or play duels, look up answers during a live duel, manipulate rankings, bypass signup protections, impersonate someone, or harass other players. HoopDuels may suspend or remove accounts that violate these rules.

Your account

Keep your password private. You can delete your account from your profile. The Privacy Notice explains what happens to match records after deletion.

Support and corrections

Email hoopduels@outlook.com for account help, to report abuse, or to report an incorrect teammate connection. For corrections, include both player names and the team or season involved.
